# Odd Shapes Gallery

A classroom art and economy game for the big screen. Buy shapes and paint, make pictures, face a snooty art critic, and sell your art on your way from the street corner to the museum.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any web browser, or play it online with GitHub Pages (see below).

## Modes

- **Classroom teams**: 1 to 8 teams take turns. Paint, sell and grow an art career, with vocab review to earn cash.
- **Arcade game show**: 5 random art games, judged by Judge Pixelworth. Most money wins.
- **Artist journey**: play on your own (no vocab), with optional computer rivals.
- **Creative studio**: unlimited supplies, just make art.

Player accounts, saved games and stats are stored in the browser on each computer.

## Note on AI features

The AI art critic, AI artist and AI judge only work when the game is opened as a Claude artifact. Everywhere else, the game automatically uses built-in scoring instead, so everything still plays.
