# Odd Shapes Gallery

A classroom art and economy game made for the big screen. Buy shapes and paint, make pictures, face a snooty (and funny) art critic, and sell your art on your way from a street corner to the museum.

**Play it:** https://mattick4-prog.github.io/Shape-Gallery/
(or download `index.html` and open it in any web browser)

## Game modes

- **Classroom teams**: 1 to 8 teams take turns on the big screen. Paint, sell and grow an art career, with vocab review as a way to earn cash.
- **Arcade game show**: 5 random art games, judged by Judge Pixelworth, who pays $1 to $100. The team with the most money wins.
- **Artist journey**: 1 to 3 players take turns on one computer (no vocab), with optional robot rivals. Climb from Street corner to Craft fair, Online shop, Gallery and Museum.
- **Creative studio**: unlimited supplies, no money. Just make art, with optional timer and paint-dry settings, plus an Arcade button to play any art game just for fun.

## How the art works

- Pick a picture (14 categories, more to unlock with cash) and choose who paints it:
  - **AI artist**: paints with whatever shapes and paint you have left, swapping in what's missing.
  - Handmade paintings can be compared with a built-in reference assembled from the game's picture recipes, using the supplies available when the piece was made. This comparison works in No AI mode and does not call AI.
  - **Make it myself**: add shapes, drag to move, use the Turn buttons to rotate, and drag the on-canvas handles to resize along the rotated shape's axes. Tap a shape to select it; tapping no longer rotates it. Use Undo or Erase shape to fix mistakes, even after paint dries. Choose a ready-made paint or add paint dabs to the palette and drag to stir them together; each finished mix uses one unit of each color per shape. Paint dries 9 seconds after you stop touching it by default, and the Never setting hides drying countdowns.
- Every painting needs a canvas, and supplies run out, so you'll need to stop and earn money.
- The critic reviews each piece with a star rating, a funny line and a tip, then you sell it or keep it in your collection.
- Buyers have 18 different personalities (Animal fan, Bargain hunter, Monster fan and more). Use "Talk it up" sales pitches they like to raise the price, even on the street corner.
- Screens, drawing tools, and arcade play areas adapt automatically to phone and computer screen sizes. Selling locations have their own visual setting.

## Art games (17)

Steady Hand, Color Mixer, Stickman Sketch, Speed Reveal, Guess the Masterpiece, Price Is Right, Shape Spotter, Art Auction, Shape Builder, Copycat, Mirror Me, One-Line Wonder, Paint by Number Race, Picture Puzzle (put together 20 pieces; background-only pieces can go anywhere), Pixel Mystery, Pixel Artist and Treasure Dig.

## Player accounts

Each player can make their own account on the start screen ("Who's playing?"). Accounts keep their own saved game and lifetime stats: pictures made, games played, money earned, art sold, journey days and more.

Accounts and saves are stored in the browser on each computer, so they stay on the computer where they were made.

## Note on AI features

The AI art critic, AI artist and AI judge only work when the game is opened as a Claude artifact. Everywhere else (like GitHub Pages), the game automatically uses built-in scoring instead, so everything still plays. To disable AI in a Claude artifact too, turn on **No AI** in Settings; AI artwork and judging then use the built-in alternatives. The picture-library comparison for handmade work is built from the game's existing picture recipes and works without AI.
